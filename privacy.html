1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
  • Profile information: photos, bio, lifestyle preferences, relationship goals, and anything else you choose to add.
  • Location: approximate, city-level location, used to show you nearby matches.
  • Messages and calls: text and voice messages you send to matches, and the connection data needed to place voice or video calls.
  • Usage data: likes, matches, profile views, and how you interact with the App.
  • Purchases: your subscription and in-app purchase history. We never see your payment card details; payments are handled by Google Play or Apple.
  • Device data: push notification tokens and, for ads, your device's advertising ID.

2. How we use your information

We use it to run the App's core features: creating your profile, showing you potential matches, enabling messaging and calls, sending notifications, enforcing safety features such as reporting and blocking, verifying your account, and processing purchases for premium features.

3. Google and Facebook sign-in

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from that service. We do not post on your behalf and we do not read your friends list or other Facebook data. You can also create an account with email or phone number instead.

To remove IDYLLE's access from your Facebook account, follow the steps on our data deletion page.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data with service providers that help us operate the App. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

  • CloudinaryPhoto and voice-message storage and delivery

  • Google Firebase and ExpoPush notifications
  • Daily.coVoice and video calls
  • RevenueCat, Google Play, AppleSubscriptions and in-app purchases
  • Neon and RenderDatabase and server hosting
  • Google AdMobAdvertising (see below)

We may also disclose information when the law requires it or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Advertising

Free accounts see ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and general usage and device information to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android or iOS settings. Gold subscribers see fewer ads, as described in the App.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Your account is deactivated immediately and is no longer visible to other users. You can also ask us to remove all your data by following our data deletion instructions. We complete deletion within 30 days, except for data we must keep longer to meet legal obligations or prevent abuse.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To use these rights, email idylleapp01@gmail.com.

8. Age requirement

IDYLLE is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we delete such accounts when we find them.

9. Security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes in the App, and the date at the top of this page will change.
